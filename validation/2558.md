#### F-001 — error — Lines 258–262

**Changed content:** `> Exclusion list MUST be version-scoped.` and two related mandatory policies.

**Rule:** `editorial-conventions-09` — Writing Specification Content & Review Guidelines → Editorial Conventions; position 9. “Normative conditions and exceptions MUST be expressed as requirements rather than embedded inside Notes.”

**Engineering context:** A Note communicates informative context. These three statements instead define merge-governance constraints for exclusion-list artifacts.

**Problem:** Lines 258, 260, and 262 place mandatory `MUST` policies inside the Note that begins at line 253.

**Impact:** Rendering mandatory policy as informative Note content obscures its enforceability and conflicts with the repository’s distinction between explanatory text and requirements.

**Behavioral effect of the correction:** No schema or normative behavior change.

**Suggested correction:** End the Note after the `AdjustedFail` explanation at line 256 and move all three exclusion-list policies outside the blockquote. An exact suggestion block is omitted because the combined replacement would also resolve F-002 and duplicates existing feedback.

**Corroborated by:** [review comment 3652739742](https://github.com/FinOps-Open-Cost-and-Usage-Spec/FOCUS_Spec/pull/2558#discussion_r3652739742).

#### F-002 — error — Lines 258–262

**Changed content:** Three separate `MUST` clauses are blockquoted as prose instead of requirement bullets.

**Rule:** `normative-language-requirements-03` — Writing Specification Content & Review Guidelines → Normative Language & Requirements; position 3. “Use bulleted lists. Each bullet MUST express exactly one verifiable state.”

**Engineering context:** Each clause describes an independently testable property: version scope, targeting breadth, and Task Force approval.

**Problem:** The clauses are separate blockquote paragraphs rather than three `*`-marked requirements.

**Impact:** The document loses the explicit one-obligation-per-bullet structure used for mechanically and human-verifiable requirements.

**Behavioral effect of the correction:** No schema or normative behavior change.

**Suggested correction:** After moving the clauses outside the Note, format each as an asterisk bullet, preserving one obligation per bullet. An exact suggestion block is omitted because it would also resolve F-001 and reproduces an existing reviewer correction.

**Corroborated by:** [review comment 3652739742](https://github.com/FinOps-Open-Cost-and-Usage-Spec/FOCUS_Spec/pull/2558#discussion_r3652739742).
