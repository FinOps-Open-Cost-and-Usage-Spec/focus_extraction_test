### 2. `specification/glossary.md` — PASS WITH WARNINGS

#### F-002 — warning — Line 277

**Changed content:** `pricing tiers are defined by quantity threshold ranges`

**Rule:** `validation-schema-accuracy-01` — Writing Specification Content & Review Guidelines → Validation & Schema Accuracy; position 1. “AI reviewers MUST rigorously calculate, parse, and verify all data within examples…”

**Engineering context:** FOCUS distinguishes pricing quantity from consumed quantity. They can diverge, much like an API can expose separate billing and physical-measurement fields.

**Problem:** The definition does not state which quantity determines the threshold. The same glossary notes under Block Pricing that Pricing Quantity and Consumed Quantity can differ, so “quantity threshold ranges” has at least two plausible interpretations.

**Impact:** A data generator could interpret the definition using either quantity dimension, resulting in inconsistent tier selection.

**Behavioral effect of the correction:** Behavioral effect requires author clarification: binding the threshold to `PricingQuantity` would narrow the concept, while an explicitly denomination-neutral definition would preserve broader usage.

**Suggested correction:** Either state that thresholds are denominated in `PricingUnit` and evaluated using pricing quantity, or explicitly state that the definition is quantity-dimension-neutral and describe how a concrete schema must select the quantity.

**Corroborated by:** [review comment 3701354421](https://github.com/FinOps-Open-Cost-and-Usage-Spec/FOCUS_Spec/pull/2564#discussion_r3701354421).

#### F-003 — warning (`rule-gap`) — Line 331

**Rules considered:** `editorial-conventions-05`, `validation-schema-accuracy-01`.

**Conclusion:** Neither rule requires a new glossary entry to distinguish or cross-reference an existing concept with overlapping scope.

**Engineering context:** `Block Pricing` says cost may be determined by predefined quantities or tiers of usage. The new `Tiered Pricing` entry says unit price is determined by a pricing tier. These definitions resemble overlapping types without an inheritance or exclusion relationship.

**Problem:** The captured glossary contains both concepts but does not explain whether block pricing is a form of tiered pricing, a separate mechanism, or an overlapping concept.

**Impact:** Readers can classify the same pricing arrangement differently depending on which entry they encounter.

**Behavioral effect of the proposed validation behavior:** Behavioral effect requires maintainer clarification: defining the relationship may narrow or broaden either glossary concept.

**Suggested validation behavior:** When adding or changing a glossary term, compare its scope with existing glossary entries and require an explicit distinction or cross-reference when concepts overlap.

**Corroborated by:** [review 4840824522](https://github.com/FinOps-Open-Cost-and-Usage-Spec/FOCUS_Spec/pull/2564#pullrequestreview-4840824522).
