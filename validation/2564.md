**Rule:** `validation-schema-accuracy-01` — Validation & Schema Accuracy; position 1. “AI reviewers MUST rigorously calculate, parse, and verify all data within examples…”

**Engineering context:** FOCUS distinguishes pricing quantity from consumed quantity. Like separate billing and physical-measurement fields, they can diverge.
